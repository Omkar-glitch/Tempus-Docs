⏳ Tempus Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 16, 2026

Summary: Tempus is a file lifecycle management app that operates 100% offline. We don't collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All file operations happen entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

Tempus does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reports sent to external servers

File Access

Tempus requires storage permissions to perform its core functionality:

  • Read Storage: To detect new files in your monitored folders
  • Write Storage: To delete or move files based on your decisions
  • All Files Access (Android 11+): To monitor folders across your device

Important: All file operations happen entirely on your device. File contents are never read, analyzed, or transmitted anywhere.

Other Permissions

  • Display Over Other Apps: To show the file decision popup overlay
  • Notifications: To inform you about detected files and service status
  • Foreground Service: To monitor folders in the background

None of these permissions are used to collect or share your data.

In-App Purchases

Tempus offers optional premium features through Google Play's billing system. All payment processing is handled securely by Google. We do not have access to your payment details.

Third-Party Services

Tempus does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external service used is:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases (handled entirely by Google)

Data Security

  • All operations are performed locally on your device
  • No data is sent to our servers — we don't have any
  • Files are moved to Android's System Trash (Android 11+) for 30-day recovery
  • User preferences are stored locally using Android's SharedPreferences

Data Retention

Tempus does not retain any of your data on external servers. All data (watched folders, scheduled tasks, preferences) is stored locally on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal data from any users, we naturally do not collect data from children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect or store personal data on external servers, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. You can uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated through app updates.
