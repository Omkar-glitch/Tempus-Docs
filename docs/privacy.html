Tempus Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Tempus watches folders you choose and manages cleanup on your device. Tempus uses Google Play for purchases, updates, and reviews.

File access

On Android 11 and later, Tempus requests All files access to find newly indexed files in the folders you select and to carry out the cleanup you choose. It reads filenames, paths, and file information. It may read images and videos on your device to show a preview. Tempus does not upload your files or previews.

You can choose to move a file to Android Trash, permanently delete it, or keep it. Files in Trash can be restored until Android or the user empties Trash. Permanently deleted files cannot be recovered through Tempus.

Alerts and background monitoring

Tempus uses an Android foreground service to watch folders while you use other apps. You can allow pop-ups over other apps, notifications, or both. File alerts may show a filename. You can pause monitoring from Home and change file or alert access in Android settings.

Information stored on your device

Tempus stores watched folder paths, file and cleanup decisions, cleanup tasks, app preferences, local statistics, and your last confirmed Pro entitlement on your device. It has no Tempus sign-in or remote account. Tempus does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Clearing Tempus app data or uninstalling the app removes its local settings, pending tasks, and history. Files you kept, moved to Android Trash, or deleted are managed separately by Android. Clearing app data does not reverse a file cleanup.

Google Play

  • Purchases: Google Play handles checkout. Tempus receives purchase and ownership details so it can unlock Pro. Tempus does not receive your card number.
  • Updates: Google Play checks availability and handles downloads. Google describes the device metadata, installed app version, and installed components it uses to provide this feature in its in-app updates documentation.
  • Reviews: If you use the review prompt, Google Play processes your rating and any text you submit. Closed-test feedback can be shared privately with the developer. You can manage Play reviews in your Google Account.

Google handles this information under its privacy policy and Google Play terms.
